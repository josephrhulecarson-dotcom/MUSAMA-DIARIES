MUSAMA Diaries is a digital home for the music, worship, and stories of the Musama community.

A legacy of prayer.

The Musama Disco Christo Church (M.D.C.C.), also known as the Army of the Cross of Christ Church, traces its beginnings to a Faith Society prayer group formed in 1919 at Gomoa Oguan, Ghana. The church was founded in 1922 by Prophet Jernisimham Jehu-Appiah.
