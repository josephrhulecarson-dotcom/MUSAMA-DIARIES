Our story

One church.
A story of faith.

MUSAMA Diaries shares the life and worship of the Musama Disco Christo Church (M.D.C.C.), also known as the Army of the Cross of Christ Church.

The church’s official history traces its beginnings to a Faith Society prayer group formed in 1919 at Gomoa Oguan, Ghana. It was founded as a church in 1922 by Prophet Jernisimham Jehu-Appiah. Today, MUSAMA Diaries brings its message and creative work to a wider digital community.
